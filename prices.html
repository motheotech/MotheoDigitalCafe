The small print

Terms

  • Repair diagnosis is R50 and comes off the bill if you go ahead with the repair.
  • Parts are quoted separately and paid up front. We show you the supplier price.
  • Website work is half up front, half on launch.
  • Printing is charged per page, including pages that come out blank by your instruction.
  • Devices left longer than 60 days after we call you may be sold to cover the bill.
  • Prices may change if supplier costs move. The wall in the shop is the final word.
