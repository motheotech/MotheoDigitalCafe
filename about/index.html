The story

Why we opened

In Drieziek, getting a CV typed, a certificate scanned or a laptop looked at usually meant a trip into town. That is two taxis, half a day, and money spent before the job even starts. Motheo Digital Cafe puts all of it on one counter, in the neighbourhood.

Eight services run here: internet and workstations, printing and copying, documents and typing, online applications, computer repairs, websites and design, business accounts, and training. Everything is priced on the wall, so nobody has to guess or haggle.

What we stand for

  • Published prices. The same rate for everyone, printed where you can see it.
  • A quote before we start. No opening a laptop and then talking money.
  • Free help first. The troubleshooter exists so you can avoid paying us when you do not need to.
  • Skills that stay here. Training so people can do this work themselves.
