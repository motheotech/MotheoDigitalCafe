The story

Why we opened

In Drieziek, getting a CV typed, a certificate scanned or a laptop looked at usually meant a trip into town. That is two taxis, half a day, and money spent before the job even starts. Motheo Digital Cafe puts all of it on one counter, in the neighbourhood.

We run seven services: internet and workstations, printing and copying, documents and typing, online applications, computer repairs, websites and design, and training. Everything is priced on the wall, so nobody has to guess or haggle.

What we stand for

  • Published prices. The same rate for everyone, printed where you can see it.
  • A quote before we start. Especially on repairs. No opening a laptop and then talking money.
  • Skills that stay here. Training so people can do this work themselves.
  • Local business online. Affordable websites for spaza shops, salons, contractors and tuck shops.

Who runs it

Motheo Digital Cafe is run by Naledi Motheo, an IT officer working in Microsoft 365 administration, Azure AD, networking and end-user support, with MS-900 and the Google IT Support certificate behind them. The same standards used in a corporate IT desk are what get applied to a laptop dropped off at this counter.

Where we are going

The plan is more workstations, a second printer for high-volume school work, and a Saturday youth programme that runs all year. If you want to be told when new services open, send a message and we will add you to the list.
