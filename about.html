— Who We Are

THE MOTHEO STORY

Motheo Services started with a simple idea: the people of Orange Farm deserve the same quality tech services as anyone else. Too many in our community had to travel far or pay too much just to print a CV or fix a laptop.

We set up right here in Drieziek to change that. From day one, our focus has been on affordable, reliable, and honest service — treating every customer with respect whether they need a single printed page or a full website built.

Today, Motheo Digital Cafe serves students, job seekers, small business owners, and families across Orange Farm and surrounding areas. We're growing — and so is our community.

Our name, Motheo, means "foundation" in Sesotho. That's exactly what we aim to be — a foundation for digital access, opportunity, and growth in our township.
