Good to know

How we work

Walk-in work

Printing, copying, scanning, laminating, typing and internet are all first-come, first-served. No booking. Most jobs are done in under ten minutes.

Repairs

Diagnosis is R50 and we take it off the bill if you go ahead with the repair. You get a quote before anything is opened. If we cannot fix it, you pay the diagnosis and nothing else.

Websites and design

Half up front, half when the site goes live. A one-page site is usually live within seven days once you have sent us your text, photos and logo.

Training

Groups of six or fewer so everyone gets a machine. Youth classes run on Saturday mornings. Certificate of attendance on the eight-week programme.

Bring your own device

WiFi vouchers work on your phone or laptop. Bring documents on a USB, on WhatsApp, or emailed to us before you arrive to save time.
