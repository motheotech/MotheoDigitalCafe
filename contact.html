— Common Questions

FAQ

Which services are available right now?
+

Web design and computer repairs are available today, by appointment. The internet café, printing, scanning and tech training are launching soon — get in touch and we'll let you know the moment they open.

What payment methods do you accept?
+

We accept cash, EFT bank transfer, and SnapScan. We do not yet have a card machine. For large jobs, a deposit may be required.

How long does a PC repair take?
+

Simple software fixes like virus removal or Windows reinstall are usually done same-day or next day. Hardware repairs depend on parts availability. We always give you an honest timeline upfront.

Can you build me a website for my business?
+

Yes — web design is one of our two live services. We build affordable, mobile-friendly websites for small businesses and entrepreneurs in Orange Farm, and we can host them online too. Get in touch for a quote. CV typing and online job-application help will follow when the café opens.

Will you offer WiFi for my own phone or laptop?
+

Yes — WiFi hotspot access for your own device is part of the café we're opening soon. We'll announce rates closer to launch. Reach out and we'll keep you posted.

Where exactly are you located in Orange Farm?
+

We're based in Drieziek, Orange Farm, Johannesburg. Call or WhatsApp us on +27 63 876 3337 and we'll send you exact directions.
